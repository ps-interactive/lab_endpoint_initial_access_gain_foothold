Welcome to IT Operations

Please join us in welcoming our newest team member to IT Operations this month.

As a reminder for all new hires, your initial network account password is provisioned automatically by the onboarding system and follows our standard format: [Department Abbreviation][Season][Year]!

For IT Operations accounts, that means a password like ITOpsSummer2026!. You will be prompted to change this the first time you log in through the employee self-service portal.

Accounts provisioned directly on individual servers, rather than through the self-service portal, are not connected to this automatic prompt. If you manage a server directly, please change any default password yourself as soon as you are able.
